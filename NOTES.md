# Design notes

- **Idea:** the family tile business becomes the layout. Sections sit on a grid with grout-colored lines between square "tiles".
- **Colors:** UMass maroon `#881C1C` (verify against the UMass brand guide) and white; grout `#E7E2DD`.
- **Fonts:** Bricolage Grotesque (headings), Public Sans (body), from Google Fonts.
- **Motion:** one moment only, the tile wall on Home sets row by row on load; turned off for reduced-motion users.
- **Privacy:** no phone, address, student ID, GPA or transcript.
